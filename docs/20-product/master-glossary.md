# Master Glossary and Naming Convention v0.1  
## ClaimStore / Kimosabe / Human Blockchain Ecosystem  
### Canonical Terms, Roles, Designations, Objects, Events, and Naming Rules

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the core language of the ClaimStore / Kimosabe / Human Blockchain ecosystem.

The purpose is to prevent drift.

The system contains many names, brands, roles, protocols, objects, events, and coined concepts. If these terms are used loosely, the architecture collapses into ordinary software language.

This glossary preserves the distinction between:

```text
Platform
Protocol
Role
Designation
Task
Event
Evidence
Bookkeeping
Verification
Decision
Sponsor
Stakeholder
```

The language is part of the architecture.

---

## 2. Naming Discipline

Every term should answer:

```text
What is it?
What is it not?
Where does it fit?
Who uses it?
What does it control?
What phase is it in?
```

Do not flatten specialized terms into generic terms.

Examples:

```text
Claim Caller is not merely a reviewer.
Human Blockchain is not merely file upload.
ClaimBuddy is not merely a helper.
ClaimsBank is not merely bookkeeping.
Quantum Dashboard is not merely reports.
SelfInsurity is not merely insurance.
ClaimExpress is not merely a website.
ClaimStore is not merely contractor CRM.
Kimosabe is not merely chatbot guidance.
```

## 3. Core System Names

## ClaimStore

### Definition

ClaimStore is the insurance restoration market infrastructure system.

It is the shared transaction environment where property owners, contractors, insurance-side users, adjusters, field service providers, ClaimBuddys, platform support, and future financial/sponsor participants coordinate claim/project activity.

### What It Is

```text
Market infrastructure
Claim/project transaction container
Role-based application environment
Claim request and documentation system
Marketplace / clearinghouse concept
Future ClaimsBank-connected operating layer
```

### What It Is Not

```text
Not merely contractor CRM
Not merely a lead site
Not merely an estimating tool
Not merely an insurance carrier claims system
Not a bank in Phase 1
Not a public adjuster
Not a law firm
```

### Phase 1 Treatment

ClaimStore is the main platform frame for the Season 1 operating cell.

## 4. Core Principles

## No User Clicks OK for Another User

### Definition

No user can approve, accept, reject, sign, waive, verify, or consent on behalf of another user unless an explicit delegated-authority workflow exists.

### What It Controls

```text
Offer acceptance
Terms acceptance
Completion approval
Claim request authorization
Insurance determination
Agreement signatures
Payment approval
```

### Phase 1 Treatment

This is a non-negotiable platform rule.

---

## We Help You Help Yourself

### Definition

The public-facing expression of SelfInsurity.

### Meaning

```text
The platform guides.
The user decides.
The system documents.
The user owns their own approvals.
```

### Phase 1 Treatment

Must be embedded in TOS, UI, user flows, and support rules.

---

## Evidence Before Credit

### Definition

A task, trip, inspection, or completion event should not count as verified unless required evidence exists.

### Operational Phrase

```text
If it is not uploaded, it did not happen.
```

### Phase 1 Treatment

No verified Human Blockchain event without required evidence.

---

## Sponsorship, Not Ownership

### Definition

Founding sponsors support the creation of infrastructure and may receive defined rights, recognition, access, or participation, but not automatic ownership or control.

### Phase 1 Treatment

Required in sponsor materials.

### Examples

```text

ClaimBuddy
Field Service Provider
Insurance Admin
Claim Manager
Independent Adjuster

```

### What It Answers

```text
What kind of user is this?
What can they generally access?
```

---

## Designation

### Definition

A Designation is a specific assignment of responsibility on a claim, project, task, request, decision, or payment condition.

### Examples

```text
Claim Caller
Offer Owner
Project Lead
Completion Verifier
Assigned Adjuster
Field Inspector
Support Owner
Release Condition Verifier
```

### What It Answers

```text
Who is responsible for this specific matter?
```

## Claim Caller

### Definition

The Claim Caller is the designated person or authorized role responsible for making or recording the insurance-side determination on a specific claim request.

### What It Is

```text
Decision accountability designation
Insurance-side determination authority
Claim request response responsibility
```

### What It Is Not

```text
Not merely a reviewer
Not every adjuster
Not every inspector
Not anonymous corporate process
```

### Phase 1 Treatment

Claim request determinations should record Claim Caller or external determination / not identified status.

---

## Assigned Adjuster

### Definition

The person assigned to inspect, document, estimate, or handle a claim-related task.

### Distinction

An Assigned Adjuster is not automatically the Claim Caller.

---

## Field Inspector

### Definition

A designation for a person assigned to inspect and document physical facts.

### Distinction

Field Inspector observes.

---

## Offer Owner

### Definition

The person responsible for creating or managing the contractor offer.

### Importance

Offer Owner can be measured for first-offer accuracy and proficiency.

---

## Project Lead

### Definition

The person responsible for managing project progress, jobs, orders, closeout, and coordination.

---

## Completion Verifier

### Definition

The person assigned to verify completion of work.

---

## ClaimBuddy Verifier

### Definition

A ClaimBuddy specifically designated to complete a verification task.

---

## Support Owner

### Definition

The platform support person assigned responsibility for a support ticket, issue, or user assistance path.

## 7. Event Categories

A low-consequence access or viewing action.

Example:

```text

```

---

## Timestamped Action Event

An action that records time and status but may not create evidence or bookkeeping.

Example:

```text

```

---

## Evidence Event

An event involving uploaded or captured proof.

Example:

```text

```

---

## Consent Event

An event where a user accepts, approves, rejects, signs, or authorizes.

Example:

```text
Property owner accepted offer.
```

---

## Bookkeeping Event

An event with financial consequences.

Example:

```text
Payment recorded.
```

---

## Protocol Transition Event

An event that moves the claim/project workflow forward.

Example:

```text
Preliminary claim request submitted.
```

---

## TER / SLA Event

An event used to measure performance.

Example:

```text
Task completed late.
```

## 9. Status Terms

## Need

A required item, task, inspection, compliance item, or annual readiness item is not yet complete.

---

## Done

A required item has been completed and documented.

---

## Annual Need/Done

A yearly property readiness status that resets to Need on January 1.

---

## Project Compliance Need/Done

A project-specific checklist status for items such as permits, Notice of Commencement, inspection request, inspection pass, engineering, mortgage package, final photos, or completion signature.

---

## Buildable

A project has enough approval, documentation, scope, and readiness to proceed into production.

---

## Locked

A record has been snapshotted or frozen for accounting, claim request, offer, closeout, or audit purposes.

---

## Superseded

A version has been replaced by a newer version but remains preserved.

---

## External Determination

An insurance-side response recorded from outside the platform.

---

## Claim Caller Not Identified

The system received or recorded a decision path without a named/designated decision authority.

---

---

The first shared contractor offer preserved as the original pricing/scope baseline.

---

## First IA Opinion Baseline

The first independent adjuster estimate or opinion preserved for later comparison.

---

The final verified project amount, cost, scope, and closeout record.

---

Difference between baseline and final outcome.

---

## Three-Week Flag

A review flag created when a user, role, entity, or designation performs below relevant average for three consecutive weeks.

---

## Trust Weight

A confidence level assigned to a verified event based on evidence, role, interest, and verifier status.

---

## Interested Verifier

A verifier with economic or business interest in the transaction.

---

## Non-Interested Verifier

A verifier without direct economic interest in the claim/project outcome.

## 15. Naming Rules

## Rule 1 — Do Not Use “Reviewer” When “Claim Caller” Is Needed

Reviewer is generic.

Claim Caller means accountable determination authority.

Use Reviewer only when the person reviews without decision authority.

---

## Rule 2 — Do Not Use “User Role” When “Designation” Is Needed

If the question is “what kind of user is this?” use Entity Role.

If the question is “who is responsible for this specific decision/task?” use Designation.

---

## Rule 3 — Do Not Call ClaimsBank a Bank in Phase 1

Use:

```text
ClaimsBank release-condition model
ClaimsBank-ready event
ClaimsBank transaction record
Escrow candidate
Reserve candidate
```

until legal structure is complete.

---

## Rule 4 — Do Not Call Human Blockchain a Crypto Product in Phase 1

Use:

```text
Verified event ledger
Human-verified record
Evidence-backed event
```

unless future architecture requires literal blockchain.

---

## Rule 5 — Do Not Call SelfInsurity an Insurance Product Without Review

Use:

```text
Guided self-help
User-directed claim file
Risk/protection concept
```

until legal/product structure is vetted.

---

## Rule 6 — Do Not Call Sponsor Positions Ownership

Use:

```text
Sponsor position
Founding participation
Defined rights
Seasonal rights
Category/territory support
```

not ownership unless legally structured.

---

## Rule 7 — Do Not Flatten ClaimStore Into Contractor Software

Contractor BMS is a component.

ClaimStore is market infrastructure.

---

## Rule 8 — Preserve First Offer and Final Closeout Language

These terms are essential for proficiency and underwriting.

---

## Rule 9 — Preserve Need/Done as Two Concepts

Annual Need/Done is outside claim workflow.

Project Compliance Need/Done is inside project workflow.

---

## Rule 10 — Use “Legal To Be Vetted” When Appropriate

Do not pretend legal conclusions are final.
