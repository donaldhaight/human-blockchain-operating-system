# Season 1 Operating Plan and Roadmap v0.1  
## One Prompt Event to Proof of Coherence  
### 11-1-2026 through 11-1-2027

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the Season 1 operating plan for the ClaimStore / Kimosabe / Human Blockchain / ClaimsBank / RRCA / SelfInsurity ecosystem.

Season 1 is not the national rollout.

Season 1 is the proof of coherence.

The objective is to prove that the architecture can move from concept to controlled execution inside one real operating cell.

Season 1 must show that the system can coordinate:

```text id="bey63x"
Users
Roles
Designations
Properties
Claims
Projects
Offers
Jobs
Orders
Other Charges
Evidence
Claim Requests
Claim Caller accountability
ClaimBuddy verification
Bookkeeping events
TER / SLA
Proficiency
ClaimsBank conditions
Quantum Dashboard underwriting
Sponsor positions
```

The purpose of Season 1 is to prove that the system is real enough to operate, underwrite, improve, and fund.

## 2. Season 1 Theme

The Season 1 theme is:

# Proof of Coherence

Season 1 must prove that the architecture makes sense when placed under the weight of real-world workflow.

Not theory.

Not pitch.

Not isolated software.

Not scattered documents.

A coherent operating cell.

The question Season 1 must answer:

```text id="2lh0hq"
Can the system turn a real insurance restoration opportunity into a structured, documented, verified, financially tracked, and dashboard-visible transaction?
```

If yes, the system becomes scalable.

## 6. Season 1 Core Strategy

Season 1 uses the George Washington rollout model.

The founding operator crosses first.

The market does not yet fully understand neutral infrastructure, so the founding contractor operation helps prove trust by standing behind the work.

The transition path is:

```text id="0v9yun"
Trust the founding operator
↓
Trust the documented workflow
↓
Trust the evidence
↓
Trust the protocol
↓
Trust the verified record
↓
Trust the reserve / release logic
↓
Trust the market infrastructure
```

Season 1 should not hide the founding operator’s role.

It should explain it.

The founding operator is the bridge.

## 10. Season 1 Operating Milestones

### Operating Milestone 1 — First Controlled Claim/Project

Goal:

Process one project from lead to offer to acceptance to claim request to evidence to completion.

### Operating Milestone 2 — First Claim Caller Record

Goal:

Record an insurance-side decision path, including Claim Caller or external determination.

### Operating Milestone 3 — First ClaimBuddy Verification

Goal:

Complete a field task with evidence, checklist, timestamp, and verification status.

### Operating Milestone 4 — First Proficiency Variance

Goal:

Compare first offer baseline to final closeout.

### Operating Milestone 5 — First TER Flag / Review

Goal:

Demonstrate the system can detect late, incomplete, or below-average task performance.

### Operating Milestone 6 — First Quantum Dashboard Scenario

Goal:

Show a market opportunity model using assumptions and actual early events.

### Operating Milestone 7 — First Sponsor Position Package

Goal:

Present one or more founding sponsor positions supported by dashboard logic.
