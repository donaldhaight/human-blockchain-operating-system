# Human Blockchain Master Continuity Brief

## If this is the only document the next team receives

Donald Haight has spent more than twenty years developing a system intended to reduce conflicts of interest and coordinate people, companies, claims, construction work, evidence, commitments, and money across the insurance-restoration market. Since late 2023, he has deliberately developed the same underlying plan across ChatGPT, Claude, Manus, NotebookLM, Gemini, and Grok in anticipation of a time when teams of software agents could retrieve the work, understand the intention, build the platform, test it in a real operating company, and improve it continuously.

That time has arrived.

The mission is not to perfect an archive before acting. The mission is to build a continuously improving operating system from Donald's present business-plan narrative, use RRCA as Customer Zero, and retrieve the historical corpus whenever the team needs deeper requirements, earlier designs, evidence, creative material, or alternative solutions.

The shortest accurate statement of the system is:

> **A domain or relationship originates a Lead; identity, group, role, and permissions determine who may act; ClaimExpress turns the Lead into an attributable Offer and service commitment; accepted work becomes Projects, Jobs, Job Orders, Tasks, and evidence; qualified third parties verify completion; regulated providers move money; ledgers record value; observed performance improves assignment, underwriting, company formation, and the next version of the system.**

Donald is the founder and current integrator. The immediate goal is to make the system sufficiently explicit and operational that Donald no longer has to serve as the human middleware connecting every idea, company, task, and transaction—and sufficiently useful to RRCA that he no longer has to come to the office or climb roofs to keep the business functioning.

## 1. Founder intention

Donald is simultaneously:

- a roofing, construction, and insurance-restoration expert with more than thirty years of experience;
- the owner/operator whose practical need produced the system;
- a licensed contractor who still needs favorable insurer relationships, managed-repair opportunities, qualified leads, and a successful economic exit;
- the founder of a portfolio of proposed companies, protocols, brands, domains, cultural works, and public-interest concepts;
- the author of nonfiction, fiction, hybrid memoir, research, agreements, songs, video concepts, games, and other intellectual property that can explain and distribute the system; and
- the present decision authority for the Season 1–2 beta.

Do not reduce him to a software founder, contractor, content creator, claimant advocate, or speculative futurist. The platform exists because those identities overlap.

The system must serve the contractor's immediate reality while building a neutral market architecture capable of surviving Donald's eventual exit. Insurer participation is commercially important. If insurers value the system, route work through it, and adopt service commitments, the resulting lead and managed-repair opportunity may provide Donald's economic path out of daily contracting. If traditional insurers continue eliminating or restricting roof coverage because they consider the risk unprofitable, the alternative is to help a qualified alliance, cooperative, network, or other sponsor develop an InsurTech response using better evidence, maintenance, managed repair, loss control, and aligned stakeholder behavior.

## 2. What the corpus is—and is not

The corpus includes work from at least six AI environments, two ChatGPT account contexts, historical Siteforum materials, formation and legal documents, software requirements, company plans, research, creative work, domain names, websites, applications, source code, exports, and repeated attempts to describe the same system.

It contains many variations of names, organizations, ownership plans, technical solutions, and narratives. Those variations are not a defect requiring an immediate canonical ruling. They are the record of a work in progress and a supply of reusable intelligence.

Use the corpus for:

1. **Recall** — retrieve prior treatment of a current question.
2. **Production** — create requirements, plans, applications, agreements, research, stories, campaigns, songs, games, and other outputs.
3. **Validation** — compare a proposed implementation with recurring intention, historical constraints, alternative designs, and adverse evidence.
4. **Acceleration** — give agents specialized context without requiring Donald to repeat decades of work.
5. **Training and evaluation** — test whether a fresh team can infer the system, explain uncertainty, implement a bounded capability, and recover after a clean clone.

Do not treat the corpus as scripture. Do not make source preservation or naming conflicts the center of the project. Preserve lineage quietly because it supports recovery, attribution, compliance, and learning. Present intention and verified operating behavior control current execution.

## 8. Commercial and work-object model

Do not collapse business objects merely because conventional CRM software does.

### Opportunity lifecycle

> **Prospect → Lead → Offer → Pending Project → Project**

- A **Prospect** may be an identifiable possible participant or opportunity.
- A **Lead** is a timestamped opportunity with origin and routing history. It is not automatically a Contact, Customer, or Project.
- An **Offer** is the contractor's structured proposal and supporting terms.
- A **Pending Project** exists after the necessary interest/acceptance events but before the required project agreement is fully executed.
- A **Project** becomes Open following execution by the required parties.

Referral, Warranty, and Maintenance pathways continue the relationship beyond the initial Project and can originate new Needs, Jobs, or Leads while preserving attribution.

### Work hierarchy

> **Project → Job → Job Order → Task Request → Task → evidence → Done**

- A Project contains Jobs.
- Each Job has a Selling Price and contains Job Orders.
- A Job Order authorizes or organizes a bounded unit of work.
- A Task Request expresses a Need for action.
- A Task is assigned work with responsibility, timing, state, evidence, and service expectations.
- Done requires defined evidence and, where applicable, third-party confirmation.

This model is provisional. Donald's further narration controls definitions and missing transitions.

## 9. ClaimExpress Protocol

ClaimExpress is not merely estimating software. It is a contracting protocol and service-level agreement that translates complicated insurance estimates—often forty pages and produced through multiple software systems—into a standard format for contracting, communication, response, work decomposition, verification, and payment evidence.

### Lead-to-Offer SLA

When the system originates a Lead and routes it to a Licensed Contractor and Independent Sales Representative, the working expectation is **Offer Complete within 48 hours**. The future protocol must define acknowledgment, pauses, missing-information states, escalation, reassignment, exceptions, and quality requirements.

### Offer structure

The current model includes:

- Jobs, each with a Selling Price;
- Other Charges, each with a Selling Price; and
- Jobs Not Included, whose amounts are subtracted in deriving the proposed Selling Price.

The specification must later address taxes, allowances, alternates, change orders, deductibles, depreciation, overhead and profit, supplements, insurer-estimate reconciliation, payment schedules, exclusions, and jurisdiction-specific contracting rules.

### Property Owner authority

The Property Owner accepts the Offer and intentionally clicks **Submit to Insurance**. The system helps the owner help themself; it does not silently submit on the owner's behalf. Record actor, authority, consent, document version, date/time, destination, transmission evidence, and response.

### Claim Request and Need-to-Done

The insurer response becomes a Claim Request received with a timestamp and Service Level Commitment. The Claim Request becomes a Need that must progress toward Done.

The receiving party may be able to accept while unchecking unsupported or declined Jobs, line items, or Other Charges. The finished protocol must distinguish contractor-scope exclusions from coverage determinations and define authority, disclosure, consent, dispute, revision, and appeal.

### Project execution and release

After required Property Owner and LC execution, the Pending Project becomes an Open Project with Open Jobs and Open Job Orders. Job Orders are assigned, performed, evidenced, and verified. A properly independent ClaimBuddy or other authorized verifier confirms the defined completion condition before a qualified financial provider releases money.

ClaimExpress records events and evidence. It does not automatically become the insurer, adjuster, law firm, escrow agent, bank, money transmitter, accountant, or fiduciary.

### Task Efficiency Rating

Workflow events create Tasks. The cultural rule “Due Today” means acknowledge, act, document a dependency, or establish the next committed step now. It does not falsely require weeks of physical work to finish in one day.

The Task Efficiency Rating may compare service behavior against relevant averages. It must incorporate quality, task type, dependency time, role, jurisdiction, workload, legitimate accommodations, dispute rights, and anti-gaming safeguards. It should help participants remain above a visible service standard without becoming an opaque system that unfairly controls access to work.

## 10. Buddy Claim, ClaimBuddy, and SelfInsurity

**Buddy Claim** is a contemplated legal entity/business line serving multiple platform roles. Its core opportunity is to use better evidence, aligned behavior, maintenance, service performance, managed repair, and transparent transaction history to reconsider roof risk that traditional carriers may be reducing or excluding.

## 11. ClaimStore and market neutrality

ClaimStore is conceived as neutral market infrastructure: potentially the escrow/payment/clearing and transaction evidence layer—the “VISA or central bank of the market” in the founder's shorthand. Its architecture must prevent any one insurer, contractor, supplier, platform operator, or investor from manipulating pricing, access, evidence, or settlement for its private advantage.

That neutrality requirement is the heart of the historical failed consortium effort and must not be traded away casually for capital or distribution.

ClaimStore's permanent form remains open. Possibilities include nonprofit, limited-profit, stakeholder-controlled, regulated operator, or sale to an appropriately governed stakeholder consortium. Its actual custody, escrow, money-transmission, settlement, or payment functions require FinCEN, state financial-regulation, insurance, banking, tax, accounting, and contractual analysis. During the beta, use qualified third-party providers and treat ClaimStore primarily as a protocol, instruction, evidence, and reconciliation layer.

## 14. Case study and institutional co-discovery

The case study is not decorative marketing. It is the mechanism by which the platform earns credibility.

RRCA runs a bounded real workflow. The system records time, responsibility, offers, agreements, service commitments, exclusions, Jobs, Job Orders, Tasks, evidence, money events, outcomes, customer experience, and exceptions. Synthetic or de-identified datasets support demonstrations before regulated or private data is appropriate.

The institutional posture is:

> Present the real problem, proposed control system, evidence, assumptions, risks, and narrow unanswered questions early enough that credible institutions can challenge the design before regulated activity begins.

Potential reviewers include academics, insurers, suppliers, banks/payment providers, accountants, insurance regulators, securities counsel and SEC staff, consumer-protection specialists, and standards communities. Their participation must never be characterized as endorsement unless formal authorization expressly permits that statement.

The same pattern applies to suppliers: show the Project, purchase, evidence, and proposed payment workflow; ask suppliers and qualified providers to define acceptable credit, joint-check, escrow, controlled-disbursement, lien-waiver, milestone, or direct-payment methods; then configure the platform to support the accepted method.
