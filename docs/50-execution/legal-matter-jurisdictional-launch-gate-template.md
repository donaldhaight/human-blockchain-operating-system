# Legal Matter and Jurisdictional Launch-Gate Template

## Purpose

Use this template before opening a real legal-service intake, publishing a legal-service offer, adding a jurisdictional counsel-coverage claim, activating legal-matter access for an attorney/firm, or identifying any lawyer as counsel or attorney of record. Completion records readiness; it does not create a lawyer-client relationship, legal authority, court appearance, professional entity, law firm, reciprocal insurance exchange, or regulatory approval.
