## Genesis ClaimStore Season 1 Repository

---

## 1. Purpose

This document indexes the historical source materials used to create the Genesis ClaimStore Season 1 canon.

The purpose is to preserve the chain of origin between:

```text id="6ftymf"
Historical documents
Field experience
Original product concepts
Modern architecture
Phase 1 requirements
Legal review
Build backlog
Season 1 operating plan
```

The source documents are not treated as final modern requirements.

They are treated as:

```text id="t7jq5y"
Evidence of origin
Architectural memory
Product discovery
Operational scar tissue
Legal boundary clues
Market insight
Historical proof of pattern
```

---

## 2. Source Handling Rule

Historical documents should be translated, not copied blindly.

The modern build must consider:

```text id="3k18dq"
High-speed internet
Mobile-first workflows
Cloud infrastructure
Modern SaaS architecture
Electronic signatures
AI agents
Privacy expectations
Platform liability
Public adjusting rules
Payment/escrow regulation
Sponsor/investor sensitivity
Modern UX standards
```

A source may contain an important concept but an outdated implementation.

Preserve the concept.

Modernize the implementation.
