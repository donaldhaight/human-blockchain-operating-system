# Founding Stakeholder Sponsor Position Book v0.1  
## Seven Groups, Sponsorship Not Ownership, Seasonal Rights, and Market Infrastructure Participation

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the Founding Stakeholder Sponsor Position model for the ClaimStore / Kimosabe / United Stakeholders ecosystem.

The purpose is to convert the architecture into sponsor-ready positions without prematurely selling ownership, equity, securities, governance rights, insurance products, banking products, or regulated promises.

The core frame is:

```text
Sponsorship, not ownership.
Participation, not control.
Founding-period rights, not permanent entitlement.
Market infrastructure support, not speculative hype.
```

A Founding Sponsor Position gives a qualified stakeholder the opportunity to sit at the table during the founding period, support the buildout of a defined market infrastructure layer, and receive defined recognition, access, integration, category, territory, season, or program rights.

---

## 2. Core Thesis

The insurance restoration market already exists.

The money already moves.

The claims already happen.

The contractors already build.

The property owners already suffer loss.

The insurers already make determinations.

The mortgage companies already hold funds.

The suppliers already sell materials.

The adjusters already inspect.

The problem is not lack of economic activity.

The problem is lack of shared infrastructure.

ClaimStore, Human Blockchain, ClaimsBank, RRCA, SelfInsurity, Market Applications, Buddy Claim, Kimosabe, and United Stakeholders are organized to create that missing infrastructure.

Sponsors do not buy the market.

Sponsors help build the table.

## 5. Founding Period

The Founding Period is the limited early window during which sponsor positions are defined, priced, negotiated, tested, and refined.

The Founding Period may include:

```text
Pre-Season / One Prompt Event
Season 1
Season 2
Season 3
```

Recommended framing:

```text

```

The Founding Period should not be indefinite.

Scarcity matters.

## 26. TER / SLA Sponsor Impact

Sponsors may benefit from TER but should not manipulate it.

Rules:

```text
Sponsors may view relevant aggregated performance data.
Sponsors may support training and improvement.
Sponsors may condition program eligibility on TER.
Sponsors may not secretly alter TER.
Sponsors may not suppress adverse performance records.
Sponsors may not route around protocol rules.
```

Sponsor trust depends on the integrity of the performance layer.

## 31. Sponsorship vs. Ownership

The public phrase:

```text
Sponsorship, not ownership.
```

Meaning:

```text
The sponsor supports the founding of a market infrastructure position.
The sponsor may receive defined rights.
The sponsor does not automatically own the platform, the market, the data, the governance, or the users.
```

This protects:

```text
Neutrality
Governance
Future flexibility
Stakeholder trust
Legal structure
Market credibility
```

## 33. Sponsor Risk Disclosure

Sponsor materials should disclose:

```text
The system is in development.
Legal structures require review.
Insurance, banking, escrow, lending, securities, and legal-service implications must be vetted.

Market assumptions are scenarios, not promises.
Dashboard projections depend on inputs and adoption.
Rights are defined only by written agreement.
```

## 36. Working Conclusion

Founding Sponsor Positions turn architecture into participation.

They let serious stakeholders support the creation of market infrastructure without confusing sponsorship with ownership, governance control, regulated authority, or speculative promises.

A good sponsor position defines:

```text
What category?
What territory?
What season?
What rights?
What obligations?
What contribution?
What data?
What risk?
What renewal?
What revocation?
```

The table must be valuable because the table must be trustworthy.

ClaimStore does not need sponsors who merely want exposure.

It needs founding stakeholders who understand that the insurance restoration market is ready for infrastructure.
